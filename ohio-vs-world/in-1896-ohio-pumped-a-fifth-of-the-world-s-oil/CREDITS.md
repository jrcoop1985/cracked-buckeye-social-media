# In 1896 Ohio pumped a fifth of the world's oil

Eight carousel slides by Cracked Buckeye.

- Photos: two postcards contributed to Wikimedia Commons by the Columbus Metropolitan Library (via DPLA), marked public
  domain. Slide 1: "Greetings from Lima, O.", photo "Gusher Burning" by Trost, dated Dec. 9, 1904 (cropped).
  Slide 8: "Solar Refinery, Lima, O.", 1913 (cropped).
- Data: U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Resources of the United States, 1913 and 1900; S. F. Peckham, Report on the
  Production, Technology, and Uses of Petroleum, Tenth U.S. Census (1880), vol. 10; U.S. Energy Information
  Administration (all public domain).
